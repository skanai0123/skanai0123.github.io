Immiscible oil and water: geometric PLIC/VOF transports the occupied area and its boundary, rather than a mixing concentration. Intermediate cell values are area fractions, not dissolved oil. There is no empirical settling or phase-separation correction in this mode. Staggered-grid flow uses a pressure-Poisson matrix solve, viscosity, gravity and surface tension. Density enters buoyancy through the Boussinesq approximation, best suited to small density contrasts; this is not a calibrated variable-density two-fluid solver. Coefficients and ETA use model units. Oil contact angle imposes approximate static wetting on the grid walls; dynamic contact angles and adhesion hysteresis are not modeled. Extreme forces and velocities are bounded. Numerical interface and wall errors remain; capillarity can retain oil. Invert includes gravity and inertial forces during the turn. Geometric VOF method

Illustrative 2D flow with a pressure-Poisson matrix solve and conservative oil transport, plus empirical density-driven settling. This is not a calibrated two-fluid Navier–Stokes solver. Parameters and ETA use model units. Buoyancy −1 cancels gravity; lower values reverse it. Fluid velocity, relative settling speed and interface compression are bounded at extreme settings; high playback speeds depend on device performance. Rotation uses gravity and inertial forces in the vessel frame; this remains an illustrative 2D model. Oil is drawn with a sharp 50% concentration boundary; this display does not remove numerical mixing or change the oil mass.
